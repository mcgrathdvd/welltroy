REVIEW BEFORE COMPARING TOTALS
- Compare the same inner-bag version and outer-pack combination.
- Keep PE/PVC, thickness conventions, usable space, finish and zipper details
  explicit. A frosted or clear finish alone does not identify the construction.
- Keep pieces per bag version separate from cartons and order combinations.
- List setup, sample, packing and delivery items once for their stated scope.
- Keep different currencies separate until a comparison basis is agreed.
- Mark an unknown charge or unchecked specification as pending, not zero.
- A combined order total is not the unit price of one component bag.
- Record sample references and resolve differences in a revised written quote.
