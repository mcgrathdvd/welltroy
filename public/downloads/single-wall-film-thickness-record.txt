One wall means one bag panel; it can contain bonded material layers. Front and
back together, folds, seam overlaps and closures are different constructions.
This record does not supply a default tolerance or sampling plan. Its blank rows
do not prescribe the number of readings. Confirm a method suitable for the actual
film and surface. Bubble exterior film, liner and overall thickness including
bubbles require separate definitions; this record gives no bubble thickness value
or cushioning rating. Keep the completed record with the specification and sample.
