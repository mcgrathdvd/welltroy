BEFORE COMPARING TOTALS
Resolve product, sample, packing, destination and delivery-scope differences.
Keep currencies separate unless you explicitly agree a conversion basis.
Mark an unknown fee as pending, never as zero.
Do not treat a provisional total as a final delivered cost while charges remain open.
Delivery of empty bags to your business is separate from postage for filled parcels.
Keep the reviewed quote with the approved sample and specification version.
