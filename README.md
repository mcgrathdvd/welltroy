# Welltroy Cloudflare Pages Site

Astro static website generated for `welltroy.com` and Cloudflare Pages.

## Included pages

- Homepage
- Product overview
- 18 product detail pages: the original 10 catalogue ranges plus 8 additional listings
- Request a Quote page
- Industries, Why Welltroy, About and FAQ pages
- Buyer review excerpts without public screenshot links
- Supplied factory photographs and buyer feedback integrated into homepage and supporting pages
- Responsive, wide-screen homepage hero using an illustrative AI-assisted packaging composite based on the supplied product range
- Mailer selection buying guide
- 404 page
- XML sitemap, robots.txt and llms.txt
- Downloadable original 2025 product catalogue, with the matching source page on each product page
- Product image enlargement, procurement details, and social sharing images
- Ten matching Welltroy product branding illustrations, generated with the built-in image generator. Source PNGs are in `src/assets/products/`; Astro creates responsive WebP versions during the build.
- Seven supplied high-resolution production images are in `src/assets/factory/`. All production displays use responsive WebP images and link to a larger view; captions make no factory ownership claim.
- Four supplied use-case illustrations are in `src/assets/industries/`, mapped to apparel, beauty, fulfillment and gifts. Square responsive WebP images preserve the full product and branding on desktop and mobile.
- Mobile navigation uses a content-height dropdown ending at the quote button. On mobile and tablet, homepage copy overlays a subdued product image background; product cards show names before category labels.
- Desktop navigation has hover-to-open dropdowns (with click and keyboard support) with product, industry, review and buyer resource links. The gold header quote button and floating WhatsApp/quote links support quick enquiries. Mobile hero text uses more spacing, a two-column category grid and full-width buttons.

## Local build

```powershell
pnpm install
pnpm build
```

The generated site is written to `dist/`.

## Local preview

```powershell
pnpm dev
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:4173`.

## Cloudflare Pages settings

- Framework preset: Astro
- Build command: `pnpm build`
- Build output directory: `dist`
- Root directory: leave blank if this folder is the repository root
- Production branch: normally `main`

The included `wrangler.toml` declares `dist` as the Pages output directory.

## Logo

The header, footer and browser icon use the transparent SVG reconstructed from the supplied vector PDF. Its letterforms remain editable vector paths.

Add `welltroy.com` and optionally `www.welltroy.com` under **Workers & Pages → your project → Custom domains**. Choose one hostname as canonical and redirect the other at Cloudflare level.

## Quote form

The quote form posts to `/api/quote`, a Cloudflare Pages Function using Tencent enterprise email SMTP over TLS. Input and Turnstile are validated on the server; the recipient is fixed to info@welltroy.com. Buyer email is used only as Reply-To. SMTP credentials never reach the browser. Success means SMTP acceptance, not guaranteed inbox delivery.

See `DEPLOY-EMAIL.md` for required configuration and deployment. Without configuration, the form shows fallback contact information. Static Astro preview does not execute Pages Functions.

## Content requiring confirmation

Before adding public claims, confirm:

- Material and film thickness / paper gsm
- Printing methods and limits
- Packing and shipping data
- Environmental, food-contact and other certifications
- Thermal label sales unit and format

The supplied starting MOQ is 500 pieces for eligible bags and mailers, and 500 rolls for honeycomb paper. Samples are free, and bulk production is typically 15–20 days after payment. Payment by T/T is accepted. The source catalogue is from 2025; its sizes and product claims should be confirmed in the current quotation. It contains product images and size tables but no verifiable detail close-up photography. Factory images are presented without an ownership claim.

## Additional product listings

Eight listings use the final user-selected images. Six primary images have Welltroy branding edited with built-in Imagegen; kraft padded mailers and honeycomb paper rolls retain their original colors and supplied photographs. Source images are in `src/assets/new-products/`; responsive WebP versions are generated by Astro. Original catalogue products and images are retained. See `NEW-PRODUCT-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md` for the selected edit prompts.

Only the original 10 catalogue products link to a 2025 catalogue source page. New listings use product details and quotation guidance. All 18 entries are available in the enquiry selector; the Cloudflare function imports pure product metadata, without Astro image modules.

The eight additional product cards are shown only on the Products page. Kraft padded mailer and honeycomb paper images have no added corner logo. All product cards preserve the complete image and do not zoom on hover.

The enquiry form supports click or drag-and-drop attachment selection, removal and client/server validation. Up to three files can be included, with a combined 10 MB limit. Accepted formats: PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF, AI, PSD, DOCX, XLSX, TXT and CSV. Attachments are sent directly in the Tencent enterprise email message; they are not hosted as public downloads.
